## 4. Scroll-to-Zoom Inside Lightbox

Inside the full-screen view, collectors can now zoom in for a closer look at the artwork — with no quality loss:

- **Scroll up** = zoom in (up to 5× magnification)
- **Scroll down** = zoom out
- **Click and drag** = pan around the image when zoomed in
- **Double-click** = instantly reset to full view
- **Backdrop click** = resets zoom first; click again to close
- **Escape key** = resets zoom if zoomed in, or closes the lightbox
- **Pinch-zoom** works on mobile/tablet as well

Zoom uses CSS scaling — the image never loses quality at any zoom level.
