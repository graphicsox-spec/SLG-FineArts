Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. Here's how your information is handled when you visit SLG.art or purchase a piece.

Last updated: July 2026

SLG.art ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Sarah L Glabman Fine Art, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone - ever.

Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to serve you:

  • Contact details you provide - such as your name, email address, and shipping address - when you place an order, request a commission, book a showroom visit, or contact us.
  • Order details, such as the artwork, size, and framing you select.
  • Payment information is entered on the secure, encrypted checkout provided by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data (for example, which pages are visited) to help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your order, and to keep you updated on its status.
  • To respond to your inquiries, commissions, and showroom appointment requests.
  • To send occasional updates about new work - only if you have chosen to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To maintain the security and performance of our website.

We Never Sell Your Information

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties. We share it only with the service providers needed to complete your order - such as our payment processor (Stripe) and shipping carriers - and only to the extent required to serve you.

Payment Security

All payments are securely processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment provider, over an encrypted connection. Your card details are never stored on our website.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features may work differently if you do.

Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe from updates, at any time. Just reach out and we'll take care of it.
