Custom Framing

Helping you create a finished piece that looks as though it was commissioned specifically for your space.

Every artwork at SLG.art is available with our carefully selected Signature Collection frames, chosen for their clean, timeless design and ability to complement virtually any interior. These museum-quality frames are featured throughout our gallery and offer a refined presentation that allows the artwork to remain the focal point.

For collectors seeking a more personalized presentation, we are pleased to offer an extensive collection of handcrafted custom frames. From contemporary gallery profiles and natural hardwood finishes to heirloom-quality artisan mouldings, each frame is individually selected to enhance both the artwork and the space it will call home.

Our Designer Collection includes premium handcrafted frames created with exceptional materials, specialty finishes, and museum-quality craftsmanship. These luxury framing options are ideal for collectors, interior designers, hospitality spaces, and those looking to create a truly one-of-a-kind statement piece.

Because every custom frame is built specifically for your artwork, pricing varies based on size, materials, and design. Certain artisan and museum-quality frames represent a significant investment in craftsmanship and are quoted individually.

If you are searching for something truly distinctive, we would be honored to help you design a custom presentation that reflects your personal style and complements your home or collection.

Please contact us at Hello@SLG.art for a complimentary custom framing consultation.

Frame Collections

Three Levels of Presentation

Signature Collection

Included framing options

Our curated Signature Collection is included with every framed artwork and features elegant, contemporary designs that beautifully complement the artwork while blending effortlessly into a wide range of interiors.

Designer Collection

Premium handcrafted upgrades

For those seeking an elevated presentation, our Designer Collection offers handcrafted premium frames featuring luxury hardwoods, floating museum profiles, metallic finishes, and artisan detailing. These frames are available as custom upgrades and are individually quoted.

Collector's Reserve

Bespoke artisan frames — by quotation

For extraordinary spaces, we offer a limited selection of bespoke museum-quality framing options created by some of the industry's finest artisans. These heirloom presentations are designed for discerning collectors who desire an exceptional, gallery-level finish. Pricing is available upon request.
