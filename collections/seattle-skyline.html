Home

Seattle Skyline · Washington, USA

Collector's Reserve · Signed & Numbered

Photographed on Super Bowl Sunday in my hometown of Seattle, Washington, Home is one of the most personal images in my collection. Created from 36 individual photographs meticulously stitched together into a single ultra-high-resolution panorama, this artwork showcases the iconic Seattle skyline, the Space Needle, Elliott Bay, and the majestic Mount Rainier illuminated beneath a dramatic Pacific Northwest sunset.

What makes this image especially meaningful is that my mother, who still lives in Seattle today, stood beside me while I created it. Together, we watched the evening light transform the city I grew up in. My grandmother was one of the first women on record to summit Mount Rainier, and standing there beside my mother while creating this image felt like a rare moment where three generations of family history quietly came together in a single frame.

This museum-quality fine art panorama is ideal for collectors of Seattle photography, Mount Rainier artwork, Pacific Northwest wall décor, and large-format cityscape prints — celebrating one of America’s most recognizable skylines while honoring the place where my own journey began.
